Stop · Verify · Think

01

Stop

Pause before reacting or forwarding. Strong emotion and urgency can make an unverified claim feel more credible than it is.

02

Verify

Find the original report, announcement, or study. Check the named organisation's official channels and compare the claim with trusted news sources.

03

Think

Ask who created the message, what evidence is actually shown, what may be missing, and who benefits if people believe or share it.
